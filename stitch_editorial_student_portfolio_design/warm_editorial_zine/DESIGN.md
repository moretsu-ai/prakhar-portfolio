## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from tactile print ephemera: student handbooks, independent studio zines, stamped library cards, and personal field journals. It rejects the sterile gloss and algorithmic smoothness of contemporary corporate software in favor of physical intimacy, editorial grit, and visible human agency.

### Core Philosophy
- **Tactile Authenticity:** Surfaces feel like heavy uncoated paper stock; boundaries look set with physical ink; annotations and notes feel penned by hand.
- **Editorial Intent:** Hierarchy is asserted through drastic scale shifts, rigorous typographic grounding, and selective, intentional asymmetry rather than motion-heavy decoration.
- **The Physical Artifact:** Every viewport mimics a desk or workspace spread—anchored by deliberate structural rulings, physical fasteners (masking tape, wire clips), and catalog stamps.
- **Audience & Resonance:** Targeted at creative directors, curators, writers, and cultural practitioners seeking depth, texture, and an immediate sense of craft without performative tech clutter.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chemical and organic reality of physical print: unbleached paper grain, carbon ink, archival rubber stamps, and natural foliage stains.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Canvas Base (`#F2EDE4` - Paper Cream):** The absolute foundation across all viewports. Never pure white. It serves as the physical substrate, layered with faint SVG micro-noise or grain to evoke tactile tooth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Parchment):** Used for elevated cards, ID inserts, and pullout notes, providing gentle tonal separation against the cream background.
- **Structural Text & Boundaries (`#1B1B1B` - Ink Black):** Applied to primary typography, rules, hairline dividing grids, and card frames. Behaves like wet printer's ink set into fiber.
- **Action & Accent (`#C8402B` - Brick Red):** High-priority calls to action, urgent margin notes, stamped circular graphics, and active selection rings. Evokes red correcting pencil and archival wax seals.
- **Secondary Annotation (`#8FA58A` - Soft Sage):** Muted metadata tags, category filters, approved badges, and quiet contextual chips. Provides botanical calm against harsh ink.
- **Secondary Text & Captions (`#6B655C` - Warm Grey):** Supporting body copy, dates, catalog IDs, table rules, and footers. Retains strong readability while keeping page contrast soft and human.
- **Tape & Ephemera (`#E6DFD1` - Masking Tape Ochre):** Translucent paper tabs and taped corner overlays with low-contrast borders.

## Typography

The typographical pairing anchors expressive literary form with unpretentious utilitarian structure. 

### Typographic Hierarchy & Behavior
- **Headlines (Newsreader / Instrument Serif):** Set tight, high-contrast, and upright. Editorial display headers should feel set like a letterpress broadsheet or poetry book title page. Use italic styles sparingly for focal emphases, quotes, and catalog indexes.
- **Body & Controls (Work Sans):** Clean, structural, and neutral. Provides uncompromised reading rhythm for portfolio project descriptions, essays, and specifications.
- **Marginalia & Notes:** When handwritten fonts (such as Caveat or handwritten script alternates) are applied, constrain them strictly to peripheral notations, scribbled section tags, and directional cues. They must never be used for continuous reading.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a tightly locked editorial look; labels and metadata tags utilize positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) with uppercase formatting to reflect archive indexing badges.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are treated as physical spreads rather than endless web feeds. Structural layouts adhere to asymmetrical column rhythms with wide, bookish margins.

### Grid & Page Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column editorial grid with a maximum content container of 1280px. Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`). Generous external margins (`3rem` / `48px`) enforce an expansive book border. Sections are divided by solid horizontal rules (`1.5px solid #1B1B1B`) rather than empty whitespace alone.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin shrinks to `2rem` (`32px`), gutters scale to `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Canvas margins shrink to `1.25rem` (`20px`). Asymmetric desktop cards collapse into stacked vertical index sheets.

### Editorial Asymmetry & Rotations
- Cards across grid spreads intentionally adopt slight physical tilts (`transform: rotate(-1deg)` to `rotate(1.2deg)`) on hover or static presentation to emulate sheets placed by hand.
- Secondary annotations drift into the canvas margins via negative offsets, overlapping borders, or anchoring to corner edges.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids digital drop shadows, ambient blur gradients, and layered translucency. Depth is expressed purely through physical layering techniques.

### Tangible Elevation Tactics
- **Ink Ruling (1.5px Solid):** All cards, windows, and panels use an explicit `1.5px solid #1B1B1B` border. Visual boundaries are deliberate and tactile.
- **Hard Cast Shadows:** Elevation is created strictly with flat, zero-blur hard offsets:
  - Low elevation: `2px 2px 0px #1B1B1B`
  - Floating card / Hover: `4px 4px 0px #1B1B1B`
  - Drastic offset / Active state: `1px 1px 0px #1B1B1B` (depressed into the surface)
- **Tape & Stamped Fasteners:** Cards leverage pseudo-element masking tape strips at corners (semi-opaque `#E6DFD1` rectangles with jagged SVG edges) or thin SVG paperclip pins to visually anchor floating layers to the cream canvas.
- **Surface Layering:** Stacking order relies on `#F2EDE4` (canvas), `#FAF7F2` (top paper sheet), and `#C8402B` (accent stamps and tags).

## Shapes

The shape system evokes trimmed booklet stock and stamped card edges.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Base Radii:** Standard component surfaces, cards, and buttons use a consistent `6px` radius (`0.375rem`), mimicking the subtle corner wear of trimmed booklet cardstock.
- **Pill Tags:** Metadata labels and badge counters use small fully rounded capsules (`9999px`) to contrast sharply against rectangular cards, acting like circular sticky labels or library catalog punches.
- **Irregular Borders:** Selected highlight elements or stamped seals employ hand-cut SVG clip-paths, subtle rotation trims, or roughened corner radii (`5px 7px 6px 8px`) to simulate imperfect production.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** Background `#C8402B`, text `#FAF7F2`, border `1.5px solid #1B1B1B`, border radius `6px`. Flat shadow `3px 3px 0px #1B1B1B`. Hover shifts position `translate(-1px, -1px)` with shadow expanding to `4px 4px 0px #1B1B1B`. Active/press state translates `translate(2px, 2px)` with shadow dropping to `1px 1px 0px #1B1B1B`.
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** Background `#FAF7F2`, text `#1B1B1B`, border `1.5px solid #1B1B1B`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #1B1B1B`.
- **Ghost Action:** Text `#1B1B1B` with an irregular hand-drawn or dotted bottom underline (`1.5px solid #C8402B`), zero background, zero shadow.

### Cards & Project Folios
- **Student ID / Zine Card:** Background `#FAF7F2`, border `1.5px solid #1B1B1B`, radius `6px`, with hard shadow `3px 3px 0px #1B1B1B`. 
- **Header Badge:** Contains a serialized ID index in `label-sm` (e.g., `REF. 042 // ISSUE B`) separated by a `1.5px` ink rule.
- **Asymmetric Tilt:** Certain cards feature alternate static rotations (`transform: rotate(-0.75deg)` or `transform: rotate(0.85deg)`), snapping to `rotate(0deg)` smoothly on user focus or hover.

### Tags & Chips
- **Category Chip:** Background `#8FA58A`, text `#1B1B1B`, border `1px solid #1B1B1B`, padding `2px 8px`, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Archival Stamp:** Circular or pill badge rotated at `-6deg`, text `#C8402B`, border `1.5px dashed #C8402B`, transparent background.

### Form Inputs & Selection
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FAF7F2`, border `1.5px solid #1B1B1B`, radius `6px`, padding `12px 16px`. Focus state applies an outline ring in `#C8402B` with a `2px 2px 0px #1B1B1B` hard shadow. Placeholder text set in `#6B655C`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom square (`18x18px`) with `1.5px solid #1B1B1B` border and `3px` radius. Checked state fills with `#C8402B` displaying a crisp white ink tick mark.

### Lists & Tables
- **Editorial Ledger:** Unordered lists omit standard bullets in favor of manual numbered prefixes (`[01]`, `[02]`) or inked dash markers (`—`). List items are separated by `1px dashed #6B655C` dividers.

### Ephemera Details
- **Masking Tape Strips:** Reusable overlay header banner styling using `#E6DFD1` at 85% opacity, applied across card joints to anchor imagery.
- **Annotation Arrows & Scribbles:** SVG ink paths styled in `#C8402B` paired with margin notes set in `annotation` styling to call out key portfolio details.
